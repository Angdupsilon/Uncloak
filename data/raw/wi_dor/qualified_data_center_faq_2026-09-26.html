  1. How does a qualified data center receive certification?

    A business must apply with the Wisconsin Economic Development Center (WEDC). The qualified data center must meet specific minimum investments to be eligible for certification (sec. 238.40, Wis. Stats.)

    Additional information about the application and certification process is on WEDC's website on their Data Center Sales and Use Tax Exemption page.

  3. What property is eligible for the Wisconsin sales and use tax exemption for a qualified data center?

    Additional information about the Wisconsin sales and use tax exemption for qualified data centers is in Fact Sheet 2114, Sales and Use Tax Exemption for Qualified Data Centers.

  4. How do purchasers claim the sales and use tax exemption?

    Businesses and contractors purchasing building materials, equipment and other property eligible for the exemption must provide their supplier with one of the following fully completed Wisconsin Sales and Use Tax Exemption Certificates:

    • Form S-211: Check the box for "other exemptions provided by law" and enter "qualified data center [enter the name of the qualified data center]."
    • S-211E: Check the box for "Qualified Data Center – [name of the qualified data center]."

    If the property purchased exempt is later used in a taxable manner, the contractor is liable for use tax on the purchase price of those items.
